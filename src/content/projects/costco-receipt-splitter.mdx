## What it does

Paste a Costco receipt as text, or upload a photo for OCR. The app groups discounts and fees with the items they belong to, then splits each item among the people you choose.

## Key features

- Receipt parsing from pasted text or a photo
- Discounts and fees grouped automatically with their parent items
- Exact money math with `decimal.js-light`, so no floating-point rounding errors
- Correct handling of taxable and non-taxable items and CRV fees
- Equal splits, or splits by parts, with live dollar amounts as you assign
- A table view of all items, and a flashcard mode that steps through them one at a time
