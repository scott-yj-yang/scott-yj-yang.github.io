## What it does

Gradescope accepts late submissions but does not penalize them, and its built-in "Post Grades to Canvas" posts the raw score. LatePenalty applies the course's late policy when grades are published from Gradescope to Canvas, with a custom message telling each student what was applied. It supports nbgrader as well.

## Key features

- Late penalties applied according to the course policy
- Custom messages to students about the penalty
- Combines an assignment's separate components (for example an auto-graded notebook and handwritten work) into one Canvas grade
- Installable with `pip install LatePenalty`
