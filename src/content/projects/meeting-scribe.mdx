## What it does

MeetingScribe is a menu bar app for macOS that records a meeting's system audio and microphone, transcribes it on-device with whisper.cpp, and summarizes it with Claude Code or a local LLM.

## Key features

- One-click recording from the menu bar, with a live transcription preview to confirm audio is working
- Fully offline, on-device transcription
- Summaries from the Claude Code CLI or a local Ollama model, selectable in Settings
- Calendar integration that suggests meeting titles from your events
- Notes taken before or during a meeting are saved alongside the transcript
- Recordings organized into dated folders, with a `meetingctl` command-line tool to list them
