## What it does

estrapatch tracks estradiol patch applications, estimates serum E2 levels over time, and helps plan a patch schedule. Everything runs in the browser: no data leaves your device.

## Key features

- Patch tracking, with body placement mapping
- E2 level estimation from a pharmacokinetic simulation based on FDA clinical study data
- Support for five FDA-approved dose levels (0.025 to 0.1 mg/day)
- A what-if simulator for trying different schedules and doses
- Recommendations on when to apply or remove a patch, and browser reminders when one is due
- Data export and import as JSON
- Installable as a PWA; all data is stored locally in IndexedDB
